wetch-comms

Personal communications tooling.

wetch-comms is a set of automation tools operated by a single individual for their own personal use. The tools manage the operator's own correspondence and scheduling: email, calendar, documents, and spreadsheets. They run locally on equipment controlled by the operator and are not offered to the public.

The relevant Google API access is granted once by the operator and can be revoked at any time from the operator's own Google account security settings.
