# Privacy policy

**Effective:** October 2, 2026 &middot; **Operator:** wetch-comms

## What this covers

This policy describes how wetch-comms, a set of personal automation tools,
handles Google user data obtained through the Google APIs used by the
application shown on your sign-in screen.

## Who uses it

The tools are operated by a single individual for personal use. Only the
operator's own Google account is authorized. The application is not offered
to the public and does not accept sign-ups.

## What data is accessed, and why

With the operator's explicit authorization, the tools access the
operator's own Google account data:

- **Gmail** (send, modify, read): composing and sending correspondence on
  the operator's behalf, reading the operator's inbox and sent mail to
  organize correspondence and detect replies.
- **Calendar:** viewing and updating events related to the operator's own
  appointment scheduling.
- **Contacts:** reading minimal contact information to address
  correspondence on the operator's behalf.
- **Documents and spreadsheets:** reading and editing files the operator
  authorizes.
- **Drive:** accessing files the operator designates for the automation.

No other Google account's data is ever accessed.

## Where data is stored

All data handled by the tools is stored locally on hardware controlled by
the operator. The tools do not operate third-party servers that receive or
store Google user data.

## Data sharing and transfer

Google user data is not sold, rented, shared with, or transferred to any
third party, except as required to interact with Google's own APIs in the
explicitly authorized manner described above.

## Advertising and analytics

The tools contain no advertising and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

## Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as the operator finds it useful. Because
storage is local, the operator (and only the operator) can review it,
copy it, or delete it in full at any time.

## Revoking access

Authorization can be revoked at any time from the operator's Google
account security settings ("Third-party apps with account access"). After
revocation, the tools immediately lose the ability to access the
authorized data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page
with a new effective date.

## Contact

The support contact for this application is shown on its Google sign-in
(consent) screen.
